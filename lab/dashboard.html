New to this? How to read this page

The four systems each get US$5,000 of pretend money in two markets: US ETFs (funds holding stocks, bonds, gold and more) and crypto (Bitcoin and Ether). They use the same costs and safety limits, so differences come from their decisions.

Return: gain or loss since the start. Worst drop (drawdown): the biggest fall from a peak. Volatility: how much the value swings. Sharpe: return per unit of risk, around 0.5 is typical for stocks long-term.

How to judge it. Over weeks, results are mostly luck. Look for: reliable running, smaller drops than Buy & Hold, and a high "Better than luck?" score that holds up. Scoring counts from 14 December for 90 days.
